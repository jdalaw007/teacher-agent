## 1. Subject Matter and Duration

1.1 The Processor provides an AI-assisted teaching tool ("Teacher Agent") that integrates with Google Workspace for Education and assists teachers in class management, assignment preparation, and student progress monitoring.

1.2 This Agreement governs all processing of personal data carried out by the Processor on behalf of the Controller in connection with the Teacher Agent service.

1.3 This Agreement enters into force on the date of signature and remains in effect for the duration of the service relationship. It terminates automatically upon expiry or termination of any service agreement between the Parties, unless an earlier date is agreed in writing.

## 3. Categories of Personal Data and Data Subjects

**Data subjects:**
- Students enrolled at the Controller's school (minors, primarily aged 6–15)
- Teachers employed by the Controller

**Categories of personal data processed:**

| Category | Examples |
|---|---|
| Student identification | Full name, school email address |
| Academic data | Assignment grades, submission status, submission timestamps |
| Behavioural/pedagogical data | Teacher notes, group assignments, learning progress observations |
| Teacher profile data | Name, email, school, subjects taught, teaching style preferences |
| Usage data | Conversation history with the AI assistant, AI-generated teaching strategy logs |

**Special categories (Art. 9 GDPR):**
The Processor's system does not contain a dedicated field for health or SEN data. Teachers must not enter special category data (health conditions, disabilities, SEN status) into the system. If a teacher enters such data in free-text notes, the Controller remains solely responsible for ensuring a valid Art. 9(2) derogation exists. The Processor will treat any such data identified in the system as requiring immediate deletion.

## 4. Obligations of the Processor

The Processor undertakes to:

4.1 **Process only on instructions.** Process personal data solely on the documented instructions of the Controller, including with regard to transfers to third countries, unless required to do so by EU or Czech law; in such case the Processor shall inform the Controller before processing, unless prohibited by law.

4.2 **Confidentiality.** Ensure that persons authorised to process personal data have committed themselves to confidentiality or are under an appropriate statutory obligation of confidentiality.

4.3 **Security.** Implement and maintain appropriate technical and organisational measures as set out in Schedule 2 to ensure a level of security appropriate to the risk, including as appropriate:
- Pseudonymisation of student data before transmission to AI sub-processors
- HTTPS/TLS encryption of all data in transit
- Access controls limiting data access to the authenticated teacher only
- Automated deletion of data past its retention period

4.4 **Sub-processors.** Not engage a new sub-processor without prior specific or general written authorisation of the Controller. The current list of sub-processors is set out in Schedule 3. The Processor shall inform the Controller of any intended changes to that list, giving the Controller the opportunity to object within 14 days.

4.5 **Data subject rights.** Assist the Controller by appropriate technical and organisational measures in fulfilling the Controller's obligation to respond to requests from data subjects exercising their rights under Chapter III of the GDPR, including:
- Providing a data export endpoint (Art. 20 portability)
- Providing a student erasure endpoint (Art. 17 erasure)
- Providing an audit log for subject access requests (Art. 15 access)

4.6 **Security assistance.** Assist the Controller in ensuring compliance with Arts. 32–36 GDPR, having regard to the nature of the processing and the information available to the Processor.

4.7 **Deletion or return.** At the choice of the Controller, delete or return all personal data to the Controller after the end of the provision of services, and delete existing copies unless EU or Czech law requires storage.

4.8 **Audit cooperation.** Make available to the Controller all information necessary to demonstrate compliance with this Article and allow for and contribute to audits, including inspections, conducted by the Controller or an auditor mandated by the Controller.

4.9 **Breach notification.** Notify the Controller without undue delay — and in any event within 24 hours — after becoming aware of a personal data breach involving data processed under this Agreement, to enable the Controller to meet its 72-hour notification obligation to ÚOOÚ.

## 5. Obligations of the Controller

The Controller undertakes to:

5.1 Ensure a valid lawful basis exists for all personal data provided to the Processor.

5.2 Ensure that teachers are informed of the processing and their obligations under this Agreement before using the Teacher Agent.

5.3 Inform parents and guardians of students about the use of the Teacher Agent as required by Arts. 13–14 GDPR, using the parent information sheet provided by the Processor (or equivalent documentation approved by the Controller's DPO).

5.4 Not instruct the Processor to process data in a manner that would violate applicable law.

5.5 Ensure that health, SEN, or other special category data is not entered into the system, or if it is, that a valid Art. 9(2) legal basis exists.

5.6 Ensure the school's DPO reviews and approves this Agreement and the associated DPIA before deployment.

Processing by OpenAI Ireland Ltd involves transfer of pseudonymised data to the United States. The transfer mechanism is the EU–US Data Privacy Framework (adequacy decision, 10 July 2023, as upheld by the EU General Court in September 2025). Standard Contractual Clauses are additionally in place as a supplementary measure.

## 8. Data Protection Impact Assessment

The Parties acknowledge that the Teacher Agent constitutes a high-risk AI system under Annex III of the EU AI Act (educational evaluation of students) and that processing of children's personal data requires a DPIA under Art. 35 GDPR. The Controller is responsible for completing and maintaining the DPIA, with cooperation and information from the Processor as required under clause 4.6.

## Schedule 1 — Retention Periods

| Data category | Retention period | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Student records and submission history | 2 years from last activity | Storage limitation (Art. 5(1)(e)) |
| Conversation messages | 90 days after conversation ends | Storage limitation |
| AI-generated memories and strategy logs | 2 years | Storage limitation |
| AI grader results | 2 years | Storage limitation |
| Audit log | Indefinite (legal accountability) | Art. 5(2) accountability |
| Teacher profile | Duration of account + 30 days after deletion request | Storage limitation |

Deletion is automated by a nightly background process. The Controller may request immediate deletion of any record at any time under clause 4.7.

---

## Schedule 2 — Technical and Organisational Security Measures

| Measure | Implementation |
|---|---|
| Pseudonymisation | Student names and emails are stripped from all data sent to AI sub-processors; opaque internal IDs are used instead |
| Encryption in transit | HTTPS/TLS enforced on all endpoints (Railway, Vercel) |
| Encryption at rest | Database hosted on Railway infrastructure; encryption at rest provided at platform level |
| Access control | All API endpoints require a valid Google OAuth token; data is scoped to the authenticated teacher's user ID |
| Audit logging | All access to and modification of student personal data is logged with timestamp, action, and resource ID |
| Automated retention | Nightly process deletes records past their retention period |
| Data export | Teacher can download all personal data (Art. 20) via Settings |
| Data erasure | Individual student erasure and full account erasure endpoints available (Art. 17) |
| AI processor controls | OpenAI API zero-training policy confirmed in DPA; API data is not used for model training |
| Breach detection | Server-side error logging; Processor commits to notify Controller within 24 hours of becoming aware of a breach |
