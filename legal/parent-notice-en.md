## What data do we use?

**What we do NOT collect:**

- Home address or phone number
- Photographs or biometric data

---

## Why do we use this data?

To support teachers in understanding how students are progressing and to help prepare appropriate learning materials. The legal basis is **public task** under Article 6(1)(e) of the GDPR, in accordance with the Czech Education Act (No. 561/2004 Coll.), which requires schools to monitor student progress.

We do not use your child's data for advertising, profiling outside of educational purposes, or any commercial purpose.

---

## Does an AI see my child's data?

Yes — teachers use an AI assistant (powered by OpenAI's GPT-4 technology) to help with tasks like drafting assignments and reviewing class progress. When the AI assistant accesses student data:

- Your child's **name and email are removed** before the data is sent to the AI — only internal reference numbers and academic statistics (grades, submission counts) are used
- OpenAI **does not use API data to train its models** — this is contractually guaranteed
- The AI is a tool for the teacher — it does not make decisions about your child autonomously

## Your rights

As the parent or guardian of a child under 15, you have the following rights under GDPR:

**Right to access (Art. 15)**
You can ask to see what personal data is held about your child. Contact the school DPO.

**Right to erasure (Art. 17)**
You can ask for your child's data to be deleted. Contact the school DPO. We will action this within 30 days.

**Right to rectification (Art. 16)**
If any information about your child is incorrect, contact your child's teacher or the school DPO.

**Right to restriction (Art. 18)**
You can ask us to pause processing of your child's data while a concern is investigated.

**Right to object (Art. 21)**
You can object to the processing of your child's data. We will review the objection and respond within 30 days.

To exercise any of these rights, contact our DPO at the details above. We will respond within **30 days**.
